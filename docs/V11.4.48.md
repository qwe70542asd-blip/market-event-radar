# Market Event Radar v11.4.48

Usability and information-priority candidate release.

## Homepage
- Floating quick navigation is a one-by-four vertical rail on desktop.
- Added a compact Today Market Priorities strip.
- Major-information lead card has fixed desktop geometry: larger image, bounded copy, no stretched blank area.
- Major news is classified across market structure, central banks, geopolitics and all major industries. AI / semiconductors receive a normal priority boost, not an exclusive slot.
- Diversity selection prevents one industry from filling the entire major-information panel unless the stories are genuinely breaking/systemic.
- Explicit future dates in corroborated major news can appear as transparent `news-derived` calendar events. Single-source dates remain news only.

## Event dialog
- Dialog body scrolls independently while the header and close control remain visible.
- Escape and backdrop click close the dialog.
- Large company-information days initially render 36 cards, with an explicit Show All action.

## Safety
- No relaxation of CSP or Cloudflare authorization.
- Existing direct Worker identity/schema/rate-limit checks remain in place.
